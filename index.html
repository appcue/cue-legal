Privacy Policy

Cue — Restaurant Reservations  |  Effective date: June 1, 2026  |  Last updated: June 1, 2026

Cue (“we”, “our”, or “us”) operates the Cue mobile application (the “App”). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights over it. By using the App you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

Data Why we collect it Stored?
Full name To personalise your experience and display your name in the app Yes — linked to your account
Phone number To authenticate you via one-time passcode (OTP) Yes — linked to your account
Location (precise & coarse) To centre the live map on your position and show nearby restaurants No — used on-device only, never sent to our servers
Profile photo Optional avatar you choose from your photo library Yes — uploaded to secure cloud storage, linked to your account
Reservation details (date, time, guests, occasion) To create and manage your restaurant reservations Yes — linked to your account
Device token (FCM) To send you push notifications about your reservations Yes — linked to your account, deleted on logout
Language & appearance preferences To remember your display language (English / Arabic) and theme Yes — linked to your account

We do not collect email addresses, payment information, contacts, microphone audio, browsing history, or advertising identifiers.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Create and manage your account
  • Process and track restaurant reservations
  • Send reservation confirmations, reminders, and status updates via push notification
  • Display your name and avatar across the app
  • Show you restaurants near your current location (on-device calculation)
  • Respond to support requests

We do not use your data for advertising, sell it to third parties, or use it for any purpose beyond operating the App.

3. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy practices:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, and file storage. Privacy policy
  • Firebase (Google) — push notification delivery via Firebase Cloud Messaging. Privacy policy
  • Google Maps Platform — map rendering and location display. Privacy policy

4. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account from the App (Settings → Delete Account), all of your personal data is permanently deleted from our systems within 24 hours, including your name, phone number, profile photo, reservation history, and push tokens. This action cannot be undone.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data (name and phone can be updated in-app via Settings)
  • Delete your account and all associated data at any time via Settings → Delete Account
  • Withdraw consent for location access or push notifications at any time via your device Settings

To exercise any right or ask a question about your data, contact us at support@cue.app.

6. Data Security

Your data is stored on Supabase infrastructure with row-level security enforced on every table, meaning no user can access another user’s data. Connections are encrypted via TLS. Profile photos are stored in private cloud storage accessible only to your account.

7. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by updating the “Last updated” date above and, where appropriate, via an in-app notification. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
